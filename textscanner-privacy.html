Privacy Policy

App: Text Scanner – OCR & PDF  |  Effective Date: September 20, 2026

🛡️ Privacy in Brief

Text Scanner – OCR & PDF operates primarily on-device. All text recognition (OCR), camera captures, and document exports are processed locally on your smartphone. We do not store, view, or upload your scanned text or images to external servers.

1. Overview & Developer Information

This Privacy Policy describes how SayemApps ("we", "us", or "our"), operated by Md Sayem Hossain, manages personal information and device data in connection with the mobile application Text Scanner – OCR & PDF (Package Name: com.sayemapps.textscanner.ocredt).

By installing and using our application, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Process

We believe in strict data minimization. Here is an overview of how your data is handled:

Data Category How It Is Handled Uploaded to Server?
Camera & Gallery Images Processed in real-time in your device RAM for OCR text extraction. No. (100% On-Device)
Extracted Text & Scans Stored solely in your device's local database (Room SQLite). No. (100% On-Device)
Exported PDFs & TXT Written directly to your device local storage upon your request. No. (100% On-Device)
Advertising Identifier (AD_ID) Processed securely by Google AdMob to deliver advertisements. Yes (Google AdMob SDK)
Diagnostic & Crash Data Anonymous diagnostic telemetry for app stability and performance. Yes (Google Play Services)

3. Device Permissions & Why We Need Them

Our app requires limited Android permissions solely to perform core functionality:

  • CAMERA: Allows you to snap pictures of physical documents, books, or notes for instant OCR text extraction. The camera feed is analyzed live on your phone and is never broadcast.
  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Needed to serve in-app advertisements via the Google Mobile Ads SDK and check network availability.
  • AD_ID (Advertising ID): Declared in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies to permit Google AdMob to serve relevant ads.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising

To keep our application free, we partner with reputable third-party SDK providers who may collect certain device-level information (such as IP address, device model, advertising identifier, and app interaction telemetry):

  • Google AdMob: Used for displaying banner and interstitial advertisements. You can learn more about how Google uses data by visiting: Google Advertising & Privacy.
  • Google ML Kit: Used for on-device machine learning and optical character recognition (OCR). Text processing is executed locally on your hardware.
  • Google Play Services: Used for infrastructure, in-app updates, and licensing verification.

5. Your Data Control & Deletion

Because all scanned documents, text snippets, and scan histories are stored exclusively on your device:

  • You can delete any scan at any time directly from the scan history list within the app.
  • Clearing the app's cache and data via Android Settings → Apps → Text Scanner → Storage or uninstalling the app permanently erases all stored data immediately.

6. Children's Privacy

Our application is designed for general audiences and is targeted at users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly solicit, collect, or store personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction).

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date" at the top. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
