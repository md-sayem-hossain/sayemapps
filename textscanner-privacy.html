Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Text Scanner – OCR & PDF (the "Application"), developed and published by MD Sayem Hossain ("we", "us", or "our"). This policy outlines our handling of information and how we protect your privacy when using our services.

🔒 Summary: We do not store, view, or sell your documents or personal data. Optical Character Recognition (OCR) is performed entirely on your device.

1. Information We Access and How It Is Handled

Camera Access (android.permission.CAMERA)

The Application requests camera access solely to enable real-time document viewfinder alignment, auto edge detection, and capturing photographs of documents, receipts, invoices, and physical text you select to scan.

  • Camera frames and captured photographs are processed strictly within your device's memory.
  • We never broadcast, stream, or transmit your camera feeds or images to any external or cloud servers.

On-Device Text Extraction (ML Kit OCR)

Document text recognition (OCR) is processed locally using on-device machine learning libraries (Google ML Kit). The extracted text and scan history are saved exclusively in your device's private sandbox storage. You have full control to edit, copy, share, or delete any scan history at any time.

Photos & File Picking

When you choose to import an existing document or image, the Application uses the native Android Photo Picker. We do not request broad device storage permissions (such as READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE or MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE). Only the exact image you deliberately pick is loaded into the App.

2. Third-Party Services and Advertisements

To support ongoing maintenance and provide free scans, the Application displays advertisements served by Google AdMob. These third-party services may collect standard technical device identifiers and telemetry in accordance with their privacy policies:

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID) and pseudonymous device identifiers.
  • Network Information: IP address, device model, operating system version, and general network connection type (INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE).
  • Ad Interaction Telemetry: Ad views, clicks, and basic diagnostic crash logs.

For more details on how Google processes ad data, please review:

  • Google Privacy & Terms
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use their services

3. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all document images, extracted text notes, and generated PDFs are stored locally on your device, you retain full ownership and control:

  • Deleting a document within the App immediately purges it from local storage.
  • Clearing the App's storage via Android Settings or uninstalling the App permanently removes all locally saved scans.

4. Children’s Privacy

The Application is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under 13 years of age.

5. Security

We value your trust in providing your documents for scanning. Because all optical processing happens directly on your device without transmitting files over the internet, your document data remains isolated within your personal device security sandbox.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy occasionally. Any updates will be posted directly to this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
